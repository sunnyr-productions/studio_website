Here's what matters when you're buying your first interface, roughly in order.

## Input count you'll realistically use

Don't pay for 8 inputs if you'll only ever record one vocal and one guitar at a time. Buy for the
sessions you'll really run, not a hypothetical future band.

## Preamp quality over flashy extras

Clean, quiet preamps matter more than bundled plugins you'll rarely open.

## Driver stability

A slightly "worse spec" interface with rock-solid drivers beats a higher-spec one that crashes
your DAW. Check recent reviews for your specific operating system before buying.

## Direct monitoring / low latency

You need to hear yourself without a noticeable delay while you record, especially on vocals.
