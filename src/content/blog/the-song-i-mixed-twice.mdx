---
title: "The First Song I Released, Twice"
date: "2026-11-01"
excerpt: "I tried to fix a bad recording in the mix, got mud, and released the song again."
---

My first single as sunny\*r, “Changed,” was recorded on a condenser mic in a small, untreated college
bedroom, and I sang quietly so I wouldn’t bother the neighbors. The takes weren’t great, so I tried to
fix them in the mix. Every track sounded fine on its own, but together the song was muddy and you
couldn’t make out the words.

[Here’s the first release.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8mxlsYJZuSg&list=PLEU8wp88nf33Jw6963CmSy3fWIf8yCpTd)

Later, with a dynamic mic (much more forgiving in an untreated room) and some audio classes behind me,
I went back and redid it. The second version is clearer and sounds more like a finished song.

[Here’s the second release.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uMzK5g5riKk)

The first one still has a feel I never got back, which taught me that the cleaner mix isn’t always the
better one. But the main lesson is simpler: fix problems when you record. Plugins can’t rescue a bad
take.
