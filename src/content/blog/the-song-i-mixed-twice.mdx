---
title: "The First Song I Released, Twice"
date: "2026-11-01"
excerpt: "I couldn’t fix my first single in the mix, so I eventually released it again. I’m still not sure which version won."
---

My first single as sunny\*r was a song called “Changed,” recorded in a tiny, untreated college bedroom
on a large diaphragm condenser mic. If you know microphones, you’re already wincing, because that kind
of mic picks up everything a bad room is doing. I was also holding back while I sang, so my roommates
and neighbors wouldn’t hear me.

I knew the takes weren’t great, and I figured plugins would take care of it. I treated every track
until it sounded terrific by itself, and when I played them together, the song turned to mud. The
vocal was buried and you couldn’t follow the lyrics.

[Here’s the first release.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8mxlsYJZuSg&list=PLEU8wp88nf33Jw6963CmSy3fWIf8yCpTd)

By the time I went back to it, I’d bought a dynamic mic, which is far more forgiving in a room like
that, and I’d started taking audio classes. The new version has words you can understand and a shape
that sounds like a real song.

[Here’s the second release.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uMzK5g5riKk)

I should be able to end the story there, but the first version has a mood the second one never found.
I can’t tell you exactly why. It taught me that a better mix and the right mix aren’t guaranteed to be
the same thing, which I’ve kept in mind on later songs. Neither of these is my best work, but if
you’re wrestling with a muddy mix right now, go back and improve the recording before you reach for
another plugin.
