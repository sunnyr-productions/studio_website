---
title: "Stop Mixing With Your Eyes"
date: "2026-11-01"
excerpt: "Analyzers are useful, but they shouldn’t be making your mix decisions."
---

A lot of mixing tutorials teach you to mix with your eyes: match this EQ curve, aim for this shape on
the analyzer. I did that for years, and my mixes got better when I stopped.

Formulas ignore the song in front of you. Every voice and arrangement is different, so putting the
same chain on every vocal means you’ve stopped making decisions.

Analyzers are still useful. I just look at them last. What helps more:

- Learn one pair of speakers or headphones really well by listening to music you love on them.
- Listen critically to everything: songs, films, podcasts. Notice what you like.
- Make EQ moves with your eyes off the screen, then check.

If the screen and your ears disagree, trust your ears. Your listeners can’t see your analyzer.
