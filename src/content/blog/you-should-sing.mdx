---
title: "You Don’t Have to Be Good to Sing"
date: "2026-11-01"
excerpt: "People apologize to me for their singing all the time. They shouldn’t."
---

People often apologize to me for their singing. I wish they wouldn’t.

It’s easy to compare yourself to professional singers, but they have world-class coaches and practice
full-time. That’s not a fair benchmark for singing in the car.

You don’t need to be good to sing, and you don’t need to perform or release anything. Singing is
self-expression, and it’s worth doing for its own sake.

It’s also how you get better. A lot of my own progress came from simply singing all the time: along
with the radio, in choir, anywhere.

So sing something today. If you’d like help, I teach [voice lessons](/lessons/voice), and nervous
beginners are welcome.
